What Pyrometrics is

A reference tool for public NASA research. AI answers may contain errors; always verify the cited source. It does not replace expert judgment or safety certification.

Data we store

Email, optional name, encrypted password, your chats, saved investigations, reports, theories and comments. Questions are normalized and counted for the frequently-asked-questions list. Queries are sent to the AI provider to generate the answer.

Payments

Payments are processed by a third party (Stripe); we do not store card data. You can cancel from your account.

Community

Published comments and theories are visible to users with Premium access. Insults, spam and illegal content are prohibited; they can be reported and removed.
